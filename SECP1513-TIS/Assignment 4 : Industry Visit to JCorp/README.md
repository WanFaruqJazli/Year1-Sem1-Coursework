# 🎤 Industrial Visit: JCORP

**Date:** 7 January 2026  
**Time:** 8:30 AM - 1:30 PM  
**Venue:** JCORP, Johor Bahru City Centre

---

## 📌 Overview of the Visit

Visiting Johor Corporation (JCORP) gave us a fantastic look into how large enterprises handle modern technology in the real world. Mr. Budiman Bujang led the briefing and explained how the company integrates Artificial Intelligence (AI), automation, and data analytics to streamline daily operations and guide high-level decision-making.

He highlighted several key initiatives, including their ServiceNow monitoring systems and the use of Microsoft Copilot. We also learned about their Command Centre, a hub backed by real-time data that allows for quicker responses and smarter management.

A major highlight was learning about the TRIA Initiative. This is JCORP's roadmap to becoming an "AI-first" organization by building intelligent systems directly into their workflow. On top of the tech, the office itself was memorable. It featured a garden-themed layout designed to keep employees comfortable and productive.

---

## 📝 Personal Reflection

As a first-year student, this trip was a massive learning opportunity. It helped me understand that Computer Science is not just about writing code. It is really about deploying technology intelligently to solve actual organizational problems.

Mr. Yusri Mohamed shared a piece of advice that really stuck with me. He noted that career readiness today is defined less by your grades and more by your awareness of current technology trends. This made me realize that I need to be a continuous learner, especially regarding AI and data analytics. Seeing these tools used in a live business environment has only strengthened my passion for pursuing a career in tech.

Overall, the visit was both enjoyable and inspiring. The JCORP team was incredibly welcoming and generous with their knowledge. I am also very grateful to Dr. Aryati Bakri for organizing this opportunity. I hope to eventually use what I have learned here to contribute to the industry.
